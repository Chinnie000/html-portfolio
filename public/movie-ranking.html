The Best movies according to my cat (but honestly its me)

My top 3 MOVIES of all-time.

Project Hail Mary

Outstanding cinematographic, breathaking soundtrack that resonates with your soul, warm touching friendship between two entirely different species, but with the same goal of saving one's planet. For me, project hail mary is one of the most memorable movie that had me completely engrosed with its scientific lore and space exploration. I love space, I love the colors of every emitted light by the galaxy. And to add a cute, sturdy, unidentifiable, rock-like creature, which was called "rocky", God it just made my day.

Interstallar

Same concept, but god how I just love space inclined movies. I dont care they called it boring, I mean we have different opinions of what catches our eye and takes your heart completely, but for me, interstallar made me cry. Even when they talk about space a lot, traveling the entire galaxy to find the missing half of the black hole's gravitational formula to save Earth, they never forget the real emotions people have during the ordeal. Saving one's planet is a heroic act, but we should not forget that they are still human beings. They feel scared, longing to their love one's they might never see again, and have racing thoughts of giving up, cause what can you do when things go south without anyone to help but yourself.

Arrival

Sci-fi movie again, but much focuses on how should humanity unite together and understand what the aliens wants in Earth as they descended. What I love about this movie is the realistic aftermath of alien invasion; people panicking wanting to kill them, politicians joining together to futher understand the purpose of the aliens, and of course how we could understand them? Language. With the protagonist with expertise in lingustic, she discovered that the language aliens are trying to teach us helps us unlock the supernatural ability of seeing the future, but not in a chronological sense of past, present, and future, but these 3 happening simultaneously. God, I kind of understand the movie the first time I watch it, but searching and researching its lore on internet and youtube help me understand it further, and I like it. I love movies that live a kind of opening discussion for theorist and lore lovers to dive in.
